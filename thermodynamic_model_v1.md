# Thermodynamic model v1: Burgers-resolved architecture

Status: derivation contract, not an implemented or calibrated production
solver. It replaces the scalar Gaussian ordering dip as the target physics.

## State and free energy

For state `S` defined in `burgers_resolved_state_and_balance.md`, use

\[
\Psi=\int_\Omega[\psi_e(F^e,T)+\psi_{line}(\rho,T)
+\psi_{corr}(\alpha,\rho,R)+\psi_{GB}(\eta,\nabla\eta,R,\nabla R,
\varrho_{GB})+\psi_T(T)+\psi_{mh}(z_{mh})]dV.
\]

`psi_mh=0` in v1 because the DD audit supplies no stored energy for the
collective state. The EXP-floor flow law remains dissipative kinetics and is
not inserted into `Psi`.

## Correlation energy and length selection

The preferred first route is the elastic nonlocal kernel generated by signed
Nye content. A local-gradient approximation is allowed only after matching the
small-wave-number expansion of that kernel. Core regularization is numerical
unless tied to a measured physical scale and must be varied with the grid.

The Fourier kernel must be Hermitian and positive semidefinite on admissible
signed fields. Pattern formation, if present, comes from transport dynamics and
density-dependent friction subject to this energy—not from a negative local
Gaussian density well. The homogeneous transport Jacobian must predict the
same fastest-growing wavelength as the nonlinear Gate B fixture.

## Boundary energy and no-double-counting rule

Read--Shockley energy is permitted only for a resolved low-angle interface with
an accepted Frank--Bilby inventory. Two alternatives are allowed:

1. use a coarse Read--Shockley interfacial energy and treat boundary defects
   only kinematically; or
2. resolve boundary-dislocation/disconnection energy and derive the coarse
   limit.

Adding the full energies from both alternatives is prohibited. A bridge or
subtraction term must demonstrate which degrees of freedom have already been
coarse grained.

## Dissipation

The local inequality is organized as

\[
\sigma:D^p-\dot\psi_{line}-\dot\psi_{corr}-\dot\psi_{GB}
-q\cdot\nabla T/T = D_{glide}+D_{climb}+D_{react}+D_{GB}+D_{other}\ge0.
\]

Mobilities are nonnegative. Conservative transfers may exchange energy among
reservoirs but cannot create Burgers vector. Annihilation releases line and
correlation energy into explicitly recorded heat or another nonnegative
dissipation channel. Mechanical work closes against elastic, stored,
interface, heat, and named residual channels.

## Required variations

- `delta Psi/delta F^p` supplies mechanical/back-stress forces consistent with
  the selected finite- or small-strain convention.
- `delta Psi/delta rho` supplies line/correlation chemical forces for reactions
  but does not replace transport kinematics.
- `delta Psi/delta eta` drives crystallinity/interface relaxation.
- orientation variation enters the Cosserat or compatibility-constrained
  boundary balance; bulk orientation still follows plastic kinematics.
- boundary-content variation drives reactions subject to dynamic
  Frank--Bilby constraints.

## Mandatory analytical/numerical checks

1. units for every energy and force;
2. nonnegative homogeneous line sector and positive-semidefinite admissible
   correlation kernel;
3. storage Hessian/dispersion over the full declared generic range;
4. no energy change under rigid rotation;
5. no orientation creation from balanced scalar density clustering;
6. monotone unloaded isothermal relaxation;
7. exact transfer and Burgers ledgers apart from declared annihilation or
   multiplication;
8. circular/bicrystal boundary limits without double counting;
9. grid/domain convergence of correlation wavelength and interface width;
10. complete checkpoint/restart of signed populations, orientation, boundary
    identity, and all RNG streams.
