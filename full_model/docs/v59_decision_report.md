# V59 physical-persistence, regime, and network decision report

## Decision

V59 withdraws the overbroad V58 strict-ASB certificate while preserving the
verified coupled production architecture and substantial prepared-boundary
migration. The final 900 K, 4e4/s three-grain pair demonstrates a causal
thermal-feedback effect: at 3.5 microseconds the physical state is 133.71 K
hotter than the all-Arrhenius-frozen control on the physical state's own
plastic-power component and has softened 41.54% from a true preceding peak.
It is not strict ASB because the component is a patch/broad zone (aspect 1.57),
not a persistent elongated band.

A weakly seeded single crystal remains spatially homogeneous on n32, n48, and
n64 despite a 55.67 K mean-temperature feedback effect. A four-grain network,
by contrast, develops a strongly concentrated elongated precursor at the same
fixed parameters. At the matched 2.5 microsecond endpoint its aspect ratio is
4.09 and softening is 54.27%, but its local causal excess is 45.22 K, its
61.6 nm width is under-resolved relative to the 625 nm minimum, and its tracked
heat/power component overlap is zero. It is therefore an informative ASB
precursor, not qualified physical ASB.

V59 also extends the architecture from the prepared three-grain junction to a
real four-grain periodic network. All six observed adjacencies evolve through
one microsecond with exact restart provenance, complete-event acceptance, and
the common-owner conservation and energy ledgers intact. This establishes
prepared multi-grain network capability, not spontaneous intragranular grain
birth or a calibrated material prediction.

## Final high-rate causal pair

The exact-source three-grain physical and all-Arrhenius-frozen trajectories
both reach 3.5 microseconds, 0.140 added shear, and 420 accepted front events
with no rejected event. The intervention certificate passes on all 43 common
states belonging to the final source. At the physical endpoint, post-front
stress is 2.752 GPa, mean temperature is 1226.63 K, peak-minus-mean temperature
is 261.87 K, power participation is 0.225, and heat/power overlap is 0.430.
Local matched excess is 133.71 K. Width is resolved at 0.936 micrometers, but
aspect ratio is only 1.57 and no one-microsecond same-component conjunctive
episode exists. The correct classification is causal localized thermal
softening with broad/patch morphology, not strict ASB.

The frozen endpoint is itself highly heterogeneous: 1176.71 K mean and
469.67 K max-minus-min contrast. This proves that mechanically inherited
microstructural heterogeneity can generate large spatial heating without
temperature entering any Arrhenius channel; causal attribution cannot be made
from a hot image alone.

## Single-crystal discriminator and fixed-scale refinement

The production common-owner runner was extended to one grain with a declared,
mean-preserving 1 K diagonal thermal seed of 312.5 nm width. The physical and
all-frozen n32 trajectories reach 3.5 microseconds with an exact intervention
certificate. The physical terminal state is 1385.62 K in the mean but only
0.862 K above its mean at the hottest point; power participation is 0.999999
and Nye remains zero. Temperature feedback changes the mean by 55.67 K but
does not amplify the spatial perturbation.

The n48 and n64 pairs reproduce the negative result. Terminal physical stress,
mean temperature, contrast, softening, and matched excess agree essentially
to floating-point accuracy across n32/n48/n64; terminal contrasts are
0.861578, 0.861565, and 0.861559 K. The homogeneous single crystal therefore
does not spontaneously select ASB from this weak thermal seed. The localized
polycrystal response requires evolving defect/orientation/boundary
heterogeneity in the current model.

## Prospective physical ASB qualification

The V59 classifier was registered before the new production outcomes. A strict
snapshot must simultaneously satisfy power participation no greater than 0.25,
peak-minus-mean temperature of at least 50 K, at least 50 K excess over the
matched frozen-flow-temperature control, at least 20% softening from a true
preceding post-front stress peak, minor width between two interface widths and
one quarter of the domain, aspect ratio at least three, and heat/power component
overlap at least 0.25. The same periodic component must satisfy the conjunction
continuously for one microsecond, and onset, width, and persistence must pass a
localization-specific refinement. Interval averages and exact accepted-state
instantaneous rates remain separately labeled.

The cadence, monotone-hardening, delayed-temperature, sampling-duplication,
gap, periodic-wrap, isotropic-patch, and component-switch counterexamples pass.
Stress is reconstructed after the front transaction without advancing physics;
the stored pre-front interval stress remains available under its original stage
semantics.
