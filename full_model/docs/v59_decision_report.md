# V59 physical-persistence, regime, and network decision report

## Decision

V59 withdraws the overbroad V58 strict-ASB certificate while preserving the
verified coupled production architecture and substantial prepared-boundary
migration. The 900 K, 2e4/s trajectory develops large temperature contrast and
true mechanical softening, but its matched-control and accepted-state
morphology tests identify a broad response rather than causal thermal ASB.
The fixed-parameter 1000 K discriminator produces a stronger localization
candidate, but its matched control rejects causal thermal ASB. The campaign
therefore proceeds to a higher-rate discriminator without changing physical
coefficients.

V59 also extends the architecture from the prepared three-grain junction to a
real four-grain periodic network. All six observed adjacencies evolve through
one microsecond with exact restart provenance, complete-event acceptance, and
the common-owner conservation and energy ledgers intact. This establishes
prepared multi-grain network capability, not spontaneous intragranular grain
birth or a calibrated material prediction.

## Prospective physical ASB qualification

The V59 classifier was registered before the new production outcomes. A strict
snapshot must simultaneously satisfy power participation no greater than 0.25,
peak-minus-mean temperature of at least 50 K, at least 50 K excess over the
matched frozen-flow-temperature control, at least 20% softening from a true
preceding post-front stress peak, minor width between two interface widths and
one quarter of the domain, aspect ratio at least three, and heat/power component
overlap at least 0.25. The same periodic component must satisfy the conjunction
continuously for one microsecond, and onset, width, and persistence must pass a
localization-specific refinement. Interval averages and exact accepted-state
instantaneous rates remain separately labeled.

The cadence, monotone-hardening, delayed-temperature, sampling-duplication,
gap, periodic-wrap, isotropic-patch, and component-switch counterexamples pass.
Stress is reconstructed after the front transaction without advancing physics;
the stored pre-front interval stress remains available under its original stage
semantics.

## Fixed-parameter 900 K response

At 2e4/s, the repaired n32 baseline reaches 4.50 microseconds with no rejected
events. It has 21.40% true post-peak softening and 166.73 K peak-minus-mean
temperature, but its matched thermal excess is -0.87 K. Power participation is
0.271, accepted-state minor width is 1.80 micrometers in a 5 micrometer domain,
and aspect ratio is 1.69. It is therefore classified as broad mechanical
softening, not causal thermal ASB.

The 2e3/s trajectory reaches 10 microseconds and 0.020 added shear with zero
rejections and substantial prepared-boundary migration. At common 0.020 added
shear the high- and low-rate stresses differ by only 0.156 MPa, whereas the
ten-times-longer low-rate exposure is 98.75 K hotter in the mean. The early
rate comparison is thus dominated by physical exposure, not evidence of a
rate-selected ASB boundary.

## Fixed-parameter 1000 K discriminator

The n32 baseline reaches 4.00 microseconds with 480 accepted front events and
no rejection. Its accepted post-front endpoint has 15.72% softening, 165.34 K
peak-minus-mean temperature, power participation 0.190, 1.54 micrometer minor
width, aspect ratio 1.84, and heat/power overlap 0.903. Temperature and
participation pass individually, but softening and morphology do not. The
matched frozen-flow-temperature control also completes the same 4.00
microsecond clock and strain with zero rejection. The maximum cellwise
physical-minus-control temperature excess is only 2.87 K, versus the
registered 50 K requirement; the control is 22.20 K hotter in the mean at the
endpoint. This condition is therefore a causal negative, not strict ASB.

The n32 four-grain network retains four resolved cores, all six periodic
adjacencies, support partition error below 2e-15, 120 accepted front events,
zero rejections, maximum front closure below 4.93e-5, and maximum cumulative

mean by at most 0.342 K and the corresponding selected-channel transition-rate
change is below 2.78e-4. This supports the global-temperature approximation
only for this trajectory. A general disconnected-segment force/capacity law
and spontaneous grain birth remain outside the demonstrated scope.
