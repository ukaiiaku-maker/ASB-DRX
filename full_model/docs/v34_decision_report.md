proposal.  A stable unconstrained IMEX control still generated the forbidden
filament while decreasing energy, showing that stability alone was
insufficient for the declared no-nucleation mode.  V34 uses a simultaneous
simplex-tangent, error-controlled IMEX step plus a topology active set that
removes disconnected births but retains attached motion.  It changes no phase
energy parameter.

existing-boundary transport only; it is not a grain-nucleation algorithm.

## ASB and thermal status

The five-case local thermal manager runs from immutable source `0c45d03`, a
shared step-100 checkpoint, and a maximum concurrency of two.  During the run,
the full and nominal frozen-flow trajectories remained bitwise identical even
after substantial heating.  Audit showed that the ablation changed a legacy
shadow temperature but not the authoritative common-wall flow operator.
Therefore those selective-ablation trajectories are
`INVALID_CAUSAL_ABLATION_ROUTING`, not a negative causal result.  They remain
preserved for provenance while the authoritative channel override is repaired
and tested.  Full-law, exact prescribed-temperature, and conduction controls
are independently usable if they complete invariant-clean.

Strict ASB remains unestablished.  Heating or softening alone is not relabeled
localization, classifier thresholds are unchanged, and no constitutive law is
continued beyond its validity range.
