# V48 addendum to the V47 claims

V47's raw records remain immutable evidence of the calculations that were
actually run.  V48 changes their interpretation in three places.

1. `matrix_free_projected_rk2` was not an independently qualified finite-time
   reference.  The attempt-exposure step rule does not bound the derivative of
   the tanh affinity, and two saturated Heun stages can cancel without any
   boundary projection.  The V47 finite/asymptotic differences therefore show
   disagreement between routes; they do not identify either route as the
   accurate physical endpoint.
2. The Euclidean packed-fraction endpoint distance is a dispatch heuristic,
   not a proved contraction or remainder norm for the coupled tanh flow.  V48
   disables this handoff in the current production bicrystal and uses a
   step-doubled, error-controlled finite integrator.
3. The V47 cumulative defect-energy increase was produced by pricing the
   reservoir-resolved trajectory with the obsolete scalar-order functional.
   Repricing the retained V46 endpoints with the signed extensive functional
   changes the defect increment from `+4.4286875458e-14 J` to
   `-4.9499624790e-18 J`.  This repairs the diagnostic discrepancy but does not
   retroactively qualify the old trajectory's cumulative first law.  New V48
   intervals use one functional at transport, locking, ordering, owner
   publication, and front evaluation boundaries.

The following V47 observations retain their original scope: the raw Fourier
union numbers; the one-interval n128/n192/n256 field comparison; the late
8/16-substep comparison as a same-source trajectory difference; the measured
21.352 pm front displacement; and the signed chemical-work controls.  None is
promoted here to a DRX, persistent-LAGB, strict-ASB, or material-calibration
claim.

The V47 5.14% variation of `-Delta F/(s Delta x)` is a finite-event secant
variation and is not a derivative-accuracy failure.  The matched physical
strip's n32/n64/n128 sign reversal remains unresolved until a same-path shape
derivative and subcell representation audit settle it.
