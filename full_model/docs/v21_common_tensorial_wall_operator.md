# V21 common tensorial wall operator

## Scope

V21 is an opt-in, one-grain qualification path inside the full two-dimensional
v34 recovery driver. It cannot allocate a phase or grain label. The default
v32/full-v34 path is unchanged. The sparse DD record does not parameterize this
operator, and collective/multi-hit coupling remains disabled.

The production step, finite-difference Jacobian-vector product (JVP), and
homogeneous Fourier symbol all call `wall_residual`; there is no independent
projected wall equation.

## State and units

For each of four BCC Burgers families `a`, the state contains mobile, forest,
and wall line populations `rho_{r,a}^+` and `rho_{r,a}^-` in `m^-2`. It also
contains explicit junction-product extents `j_c` in `m^-2`, accumulated slip
`gamma_a`, full `beta^p`, first-order alignment in `m^-2`, family Nye tensors in
`m^-1`, lattice orientation, bounded wall order `q`, and temperature in K.

Junction topology records the two signed parent Burgers vectors, parent line
directions, product Burgers vector and line, product multiplicity, character,
and reaction energy. Each topology satisfies Frank's vector rule and line-node
closure before it can enter production.

## Kinematics and transport

With rotated slip direction `s_a`, signed glide speed `v_a`, and correlation
diffusivity `D_m`,

```text
J_a^+ =  rho_ma^+ v_a s_a - D_m grad(rho_ma^+)
J_a^- = -rho_ma^- v_a s_a - D_m grad(rho_ma^-)
dot(rho_ma^±)|transport = -div(J_a^±)
dot(gamma_a) = b v_a (rho_ma^+ + rho_ma^-)
dot(beta^p) = sum_a dot(gamma_a) s_a tensor n_a
alpha^beta = -Curl(beta^p).
```

The accepted slip increment also updates alignment and the family-resolved Nye
state through the same spectral derivative. The independent audit compares
`alpha^beta` with the summed family Nye tensor after every saved interval.

The glide speed uses the declared EXP-floor barrier,

```text
DeltaG = G0 [f + (1-f) exp(-a (|tau|/tau_c)^n)] - T DeltaS
v = v0 exp[-DeltaG/(kB T)] tanh(tau/tau_c),
```

with the activation entropy included once.

## Reactions and balances

Reversible transfers use one detailed-balance form,

```text
R(x -> y) = k [x exp(-DeltaMu/(2 E_r))
                 - y exp(+DeltaMu/(2 E_r))].
```

It is used for mobile/forest locking, forest/wall transfer, and every explicit
junction topology. Junction reactant activity is
`rho_parent_a rho_parent_b/rho_ref`; its `DeltaMu` is the exact product chemical
potential minus both parent potentials. Equal positive/negative loop
multiplication follows `dot(rho_pair)=0.5 k_mult sqrt(rho_a)|dot(gamma_a)|` and
therefore creates line but no net Burgers vector. Pair annihilation removes
equal signs. Locking and wall transfer conserve line exactly. Junction line
loss is `(2-m_c) R_c`, where `m_c` is the declared product multiplicity.

For a periodic cell, the machine ledger checks

```text
mean(dot B_total) = 0
mean(dot L + 2 A - 2 M + sum_c (2-m_c) R_c) = 0
Qdot - Pplastic + Fdot_defect = 0.
```

The nonlinear timestep limiter is applied as an actual adaptive physical
interval. Loading, plastic strain, heat, clocks, reaction exposure, and every
ledger use that same accepted interval.

## Common free energy and heat

The declared defect energy contains linear line energy, a positive convex
`rho log(rho/rho_ref)` branch, forest and topology-resolved junction energies,
a bounded wall-order dip and barrier, a wall-partition penalty, and
`kappa_q |grad q|^2/2`. Nonlocal elastic energy is generated by the same
`beta^p` through the shared periodic elasticity solve. No scalar density double
well or fitted wavelength is used.

Wall order obeys the variational law

```text
dot q = -M_q [partial F/partial q - kappa_q Laplacian(q)].
```

The residual computes the exact directional derivative of the declared defect
functional. Residual heat is `Pplastic-Fdot_defect`, with conduction and bath
exchange kept as separate temperature terms.

## Linearized dispersion structure

The numerical Fourier matrix is assembled column-by-column from centered JVPs
of the production residual. Its structural form is

```text
L(k) = J_reaction + i k J_transport
       - D_m |k|^2 P_mobile - M_q kappa_q |k|^2 P_q
       + J_elastic(k/|k|).
```

The elastic term is directional but scale-free; the two explicit length terms
are dissipative `k^2` terms. The present operator therefore has no competing
positive-`k^2`/negative-`k^4` pair or other intrinsic wavelength-selection
mechanism. A finite signed mode was still searched numerically because matrix
coupling can make eigenvalue ordering nontrivial. Across the bounded V21 search,
no signed-polarization branch was positive and no physical wavelength matched
between the two incommensurate domains.

## Decision

Both regular full-driver trajectories accumulated more than 43 e-folds in the
leading wall-order branch, but the `k=0` branch was faster, wall order saturated
uniformly, aggregate wall polarization remained negligible, and the finite
signed-wall exposure was zero. The result is
`UNIFORM_RESERVOIR_OR_ORDER_TRANSITION_NOT_A_LAGB`, together with
`INTRAGRANULAR_WALL_MECHANISM_NOT_EXERCISED_IN_BOUNDED_LOADING_FAMILY` for the
physical signed-wall mechanism. A 64/128 wall HPC matrix was not authorized.

The mandated alternative was exercised with the existing-HAGB common
moving-front path. A current-source pinned pair retreated under subcritical
pressure and advanced under supercritical pressure while its conservation and
no-allocation ledgers closed. This is a short generic sign-discrimination
result, not mesh-converged SIBM kinetics or a material calibration.
