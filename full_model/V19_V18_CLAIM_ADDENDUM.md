# V19 amendment to the V18 claim boundary

V18 artifacts and checksums remain frozen. Their thermodynamic repair and
manufactured integration fixture are retained, but the broad physical-mechanism
claim is withdrawn.

Accepted V18 classifications are:

- `COMMON_PHASE_OWNED_LOG_ORDER_THERMODYNAMICS_REPAIRED`
- `POSITIVE_RHO_LOG_RHO_BRANCH_CLASSIFIED_AS_CONVEX`
- `MANUFACTURED_INTRAGRANULAR_LAGB_REPRESENTATION_FIXTURE_PASSED`
- `CONSERVATIVE_SIGNED_WALL_AND_RECOGNITION_PIPELINE_PASSED`
- `PRESCRIBED_DIFFERENTIAL_SLIP_ISLAND_MAPS_TO_A_COMPATIBLE_ORDERED_LAGB_PRECURSOR`

The V18 fixture prescribes the differential-slip island, relaxes signed wall
content toward its orientation-derived incompatibility, and uses the same
profile to define the recovering interior. It verifies representation,
recognition, conservation, and restart behavior. It does not establish
spontaneous or full-mechanics wall formation.

The generic V18 `scientific_gate_passed=true` is therefore scoped only to the
named manufactured-fixture gate. It is false for physical LAGB formation.

The following remain unsupported:

- `FULL_MECHANICS_SINGLE_CRYSTAL_GENERATES_A_LAGB_PRECURSOR`
- `SPONTANEOUS_INTRAGRANULAR_WALL_FORMATION_SUPPORTED`
- `INTRAGRANULAR_SUBGRAIN_TO_PHYSICAL_DRX_GRAIN_PATH_SUPPORTED`
- material calibration, universal DRX onset, and a predictive DRX/ASB map.

This addendum supersedes only the interpretation of V18. It does not modify
the V18 result JSON, field image, Hessian table, or their hashes.
